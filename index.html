Health Protocols

To help keep our little one safe and comfortable, we kindly ask our family and guests to observe these simple health protocols during the celebration. Your care and understanding mean so much to us.

Please Do Not Kiss Baby's Face

Please avoid kissing the baby's face. Let's help protect our little one from unwanted germs.

No Smoking or Vaping

For baby's comfort and safety, please keep the celebration smoke-free and avoid smoking or vaping near the baby.

Please Sanitize Your Hands

Please sanitize your hands before touching or holding the baby. Thank you for helping us keep our little one safe.

Please Wear a Face Mask

When needed, please wear a face mask when interacting closely with the baby. We truly appreciate your care and consideration.
